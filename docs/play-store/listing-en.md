# Google Play listing — English

**App name** (30): Grulla Music: Sheet Music

**Short description** (80):
Read, play and practise sheet music. Loops, tempo, Follow me and Wait for me.

**Full description** (4000):

Grulla Music turns your MusicXML scores into a practice partner.

READ
• Beautiful notation from MusicXML (.mxl, .musicxml), in continuous scroll or pages
• Pinch to zoom; two pages side by side on tablets
• Bluetooth page-turn pedals, with an option to turn on the last bar
• Night mode with light notes on a dark page

LISTEN
• Hear any score with real instruments (FluidSynth and a General MIDI SoundFont, or your own)
• The notes being played light up and the score follows along
• Tempo from 50% to 150%, count-in and metronome
• Mute or solo parts, change instruments and volumes, transpose to another key

PRACTISE
• A–B loops: long-press the first and last bar
• Speed trainer: start slow and speed up a little every few repeats
• Follow me: the score listens through the microphone and moves on when you play the right note — single notes or full chords
• Wait for me: the other parts play along and wait for you at each of your notes
• Practice log with time practised and the bars that need work

ORGANISE
• Library with search, sorting and setlists for lessons and concerts
• Import a whole folder of scores at once
• Pencil marks, fingerings and breath marks that stay with the notes

PRIVATE
No account, no ads, no tracking, no internet. The microphone is only used during Follow me and Wait for me, and nothing is recorded.

Built on open-source libraries: Verovio, FluidSynth and the FluidR3 Mono SoundFont.
