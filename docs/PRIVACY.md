# Privacy policy — Grulla Music

*Last updated: 26 October 2026 (score catalogue added)*

Grulla Music is a sheet music reader and practice app. It is built to work
entirely on your device.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash
reporting.

## Internet

The app uses the internet only for **Find scores**: it downloads the catalogue of free
scores and the scores you choose, over HTTPS, from GitHub, where the catalogue is hosted
(`raw.githubusercontent.com`). These are plain file downloads: the app sends nothing about
you, and your searches stay on the phone (the whole catalogue is downloaded and searched
locally). As with any download, GitHub receives the usual technical request data, such as
your IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement.

## Microphone

The app asks for the microphone only when you start **Follow me** or **Wait for
me**. While one of them is running, the sound is analysed on the device, a few
milliseconds at a time, to recognise the notes you play. The audio is never
recorded, stored or sent anywhere, and the microphone is closed as soon as you
stop listening or leave the score.

## Your files

Scores you open (MusicXML files) are read through Android's file picker. The
app keeps permission to read those files so they stay in your library, and it
stores, in its own private storage on the device:

- the library (titles, composers and where the files are),
- your settings per score (tempo, loop, mix, transposition...),
- small page-1 thumbnails,
- your annotations,
- your practice log (time practised, loops, Follow me results per bar),
- a SoundFont you choose, if any,
- scores you downloaded with Find scores, and a cached copy of the catalogue.

None of this leaves the device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage,
deletes it. Removing a score from the library deletes its data and gives back
the permission to read the file; the file itself is never changed.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue in the project's repository.
